Who we are

Who Is Big Easy Electrical, an Electrical Contractor in New Orleans?

We are a licensed and insured electrical contractor serving homes and businesses across Greater New Orleans. Our team takes on jobs of any size, from new construction and existing homes to commercial buildings. Every property has its own electrical system, so every job starts with an assessment of yours.

Our work covers electrical installation, repairs, house rewiring, panel upgrades, troubleshooting and lightning protection. We install and repair wiring, outlets, switches, fixtures and panels for homeowners and commercial clients.

Safety comes first on every job. We work to safety codes, use quality materials and pay close attention to detail.
