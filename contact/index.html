More Answers

What are your office hours?

Our office is open Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 3 PM, and closed on Saturday and Sunday. You can call 504-475-1607 during those hours. The estimate form on this page takes requests at any time, and we follow up during office hours.

What should I include in my estimate request?

Include your address, the type of property, and a short description of the problem or project. Mention any warning signs, such as tripping breakers or a burning smell. That helps us plan the site visit and prepare the right questions before we arrive.

How does the free estimate work?

We visit at a time that suits you and assess what your property needs. We then give you a detailed quote with the cost, timeline and scope of work. The estimate is free on all of our services, and you decide whether to go ahead.
