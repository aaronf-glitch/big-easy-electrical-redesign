What Should You Know About Electrical Licenses and Permits in New Orleans?

Do electricians need a license in New Orleans?

Yes. The City of New Orleans requires electricians who work in the city to obtain an electrical license and renew it every year. Ask any electrician you hire to confirm their license before work starts. We are a licensed and insured electrical contractor.

What electrical work needs a permit in New Orleans?

Most of it does. The city requires an electrical permit to install, repair, replace or alter electrical equipment, except for replacing light bulbs and fuses. Permits must be obtained by licensed electricians, so ask the electrician you hire about the permit before work starts.

When does a contractor need a state license in Louisiana?

The Louisiana State Licensing Board for Contractors requires a commercial license for electrical projects over $10,000, counting labor and materials. City licensing applies on top of that inside New Orleans. Confirm both before you sign a contract for a larger job.

How Do You Spot Electrical Problems in a New Orleans Property?

What are common signs that I need electrical repairs?

Watch for fuses that blow often, breakers that keep tripping, and lights that flicker or dim. A burning smell, outlets or switches that feel hot or look discolored, and buzzing or crackling from fixtures also point to a problem. Call a licensed electrician when you notice them.

How can I keep my electrical system safe?

Regular inspections and maintenance by a licensed electrician can help keep your system safe and up to code. Between visits, avoid overloading outlets and extension cords. Keep flammable materials away from electrical sources, and use GFCI outlets in wet areas such as kitchens and bathrooms.

What does it mean when a circuit breaker trips?

A breaker trips to stop a circuit from carrying more current than it safely can. It can point to an overloaded circuit, a short or a failing breaker. If it keeps happening, have a licensed electrician trace the cause instead of resetting it again and again.
