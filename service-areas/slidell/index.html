Warning signs

What Electrical Warning Signs Should Slidell Residents Watch For?

Watch for anything that repeats, sparks or smells. A breaker that trips again and again, sparking from an outlet or the panel, scorch marks and a burning smell can all be fire risks. Stop using the affected circuit and have a licensed electrician find the cause.

  • Sparks from an outlet or the breaker panel
  • Scorch marks on an outlet cover or switch plate
  • A burning smell with no clear source
  • A breaker that trips every time an appliance runs
  • Lights that flicker or dim across several rooms
  • Power that drops out in one part of the house

More Answers

How do electricians troubleshoot electrical issues?

They follow a step-by-step approach, first inspecting the wiring, outlets, circuit breakers and other parts, then using testing tools to pin down the fault. The fix may mean repairing or replacing a part, rewiring a circuit or correcting a grounding problem. Throughout, they follow safety measures to prevent accidents.

What happens during an electrical panel upgrade?

A licensed electrician assesses your current panel and the load your home or business needs. The old panel is then replaced with a new one that can handle that demand. Our licensed electricians install the new panel to meet building codes.

Why did the power go out in just one room?

A tripped breaker or a tripped GFCI outlet is a common reason one area loses power. Check the panel and reset any tripped GFCI outlets first. If the power still does not come back, a loose or broken wire may be the cause, and a licensed electrician should take a look.
