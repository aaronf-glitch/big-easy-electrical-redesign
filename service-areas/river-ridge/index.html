When Do River Ridge Homeowners Need a Professional Electrician?

Call a professional when outages, tripped breakers or flickering lights keep coming back. These often point to faulty wiring, an overloaded circuit or a problem with the panel. A burning odor from an outlet or switch can signal a fire hazard, so stop using it and have a licensed electrician check it soon.

  • Frequent power outages in part of the home
  • Circuit breakers that keep tripping
  • Lights that keep flickering or dimming
  • A burning odor from outlets or switches
  • Wiring that is old, outdated or damaged
  • Outlets or switches that have stopped working

More Answers

What should I ask before hiring an electrical contractor?

Ask whether they are licensed and insured, and ask to see a detailed quote with the cost, timeline and scope before any work starts. It also helps to ask how they will schedule the job around your day. We answer all three before we pick up a tool, and the quote puts it in writing.

Can old wiring cause lights to flicker?

Yes, wiring can wear out, become outdated or get damaged over time, and that can cause flickering lights or power outages. A licensed electrician can check whether a repair will do or the home needs rewiring. Our rewiring work starts with an assessment of the existing wiring.

What are the signs of a loose electrical connection?

Watch for outlets or switches that are warm to the touch and lights that flicker without a loose bulb. A faint crackling or buzzing inside a wall and outlets that work only some of the time are other signs. Turn off that circuit until a licensed electrician checks it.

Do you rewire homes in River Ridge?

Yes, house rewiring is one of the services we offer in River Ridge. We start with an assessment of the home's wiring, then plan the scope of work and timeline before any old wires come out. The job ends with testing and inspection, and it starts with a free estimate.
