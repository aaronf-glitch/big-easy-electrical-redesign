Warning signs

Which Warning Signs Mean a Kenner Home Needs an Electrician?

Small electrical symptoms are often the first sign of a bigger wiring problem. Buzzing outlets, warm switches, sparks and outlets that work only some of the time can all point to a loose connection or failing part. Stop using that outlet and have a licensed electrician inspect it.

  • Buzzing or humming from an outlet or switch
  • Outlets or switches that feel warm to the touch
  • Sparks when you plug something in
  • An outlet that works only some of the time
  • Scorch marks on an outlet cover or panel
  • Breakers that trip repeatedly

About the area

About Kenner

What Kenner is, according to the City of Kenner's history timeline.

Mapped in 1855

Surveyor W. T. Thompson finished mapping the future town on March 2, 1855, and Kenner still counts that day as its birthday. First known as Kennerville, it was the first town on the New Orleans to Jackson rail line.

Built on Plantation Land

In the 1840s, the sons of William Kenner acquired the Oakland, Belle Grove and Pasture plantations. Together those holdings covered all of present-day Kenner, and part of Philip Minor Kenner's land was later sold for the railroad.

Picked for the Airport

Kenner was chosen in 1940 as the site for the New Orleans International Airport, which was dedicated in 1946. The airport was named in honor of pioneer aviator John Bovins Moisant.

More Answers

Do electricians need a license to work in Kenner?

Yes, the City of Kenner issues tradesman licenses for electricians through its Inspection and Code Enforcement Department. Its license fee list ties that electrical license to a current Jefferson Parish license or a state electrical license. Ask any electrician you hire to show which license they hold.

Who issues electrical permits in Kenner?

In Kenner, the city's Inspection and Code Enforcement Department processes permit applications. The same department issues tradesman licenses, including licenses for electricians. Kenner is a city in Jefferson Parish, so confirm with the city which rules cover your address before any electrical work starts.

Is a buzzing outlet dangerous?

It can be, because buzzing from an outlet or wall switch often means a loose wire connection or a failing part. Turn off power to that circuit at the breaker panel and stop using the outlet. Have a licensed electrician inspect it before you plug anything back in.

Can I replace an outlet myself?

It is safer to leave it to a licensed electrician, because replacing an outlet means opening the box and touching the wiring. That goes beyond basic troubleshooting. Resetting a breaker, checking a tripped GFCI or changing a light bulb are the basic steps most homeowners can do safely.
