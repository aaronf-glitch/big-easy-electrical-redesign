Why Do Permits Call for a Licensed Electrician in New Orleans?

In New Orleans, most electrical work needs a city permit, and permits must be obtained by licensed electricians. The city also requires electricians who work there to hold a city license and renew it every year. Ask about the permit for your job before work starts.

Who pulls the electrical permit in New Orleans?

In New Orleans, the city requires permits to be obtained by licensed electricians. A permit is needed to install, repair, replace or alter electrical equipment, except for replacing light bulbs and fuses. Outside New Orleans, check with your city or parish permit office before work starts.
