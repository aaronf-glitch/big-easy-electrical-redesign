About the area

About Mandeville

What Mandeville is, according to the City of Mandeville's own history page.

A Town Plan From 1834

Bernard de Marigny de Mandeville hired Louis Bringier, Surveyor General of Louisiana, to plan the new town. The plan was filed in New Orleans in January 1834, and its street names honor American founders and Napoleonic heroes.

Lakefront Kept Open

The original plan protected the lakefront from construction "by any individual or corporation." That open space was to be held for common public use, and the city still treats those covenants as guidance for Old Mandeville.

A WPA Seawall

In 1937, the Works Progress Administration built a concrete seawall for Mandeville as a New Deal project. The city's history says it was built to hold back storm surge and the steady erosion of the shore.

Around town

Things To Do

Old Mandeville's lakefront, trail and music hall sit close together. The details come from the city, the state encyclopedia and the Northshore tourism office.

  • Mandeville Trailhead The town's former depot on Lafitte Street is now a cultural interpretive center beside the Tammany Trace, with a museum, concerts and a weekend market.
  • Dew Drop Jazz Hall An African American benevolent society built this hall at 430 Lamarque Street in 1895, and it still hosts concerts as a city-owned venue.
  • Lakeshore Drive Lakefront homes dating to the 1830s and 1840s remain along this drive beside Lake Pontchartrain.
  • Fontainebleau State Park The ruin of Marigny de Mandeville's sugar mill remains visible inside this state park on the lake's north shore.

Interesting and Fun Facts

Small details from Mandeville's history and its city rules.

Frequently Asked Questions About Electrical Permits and Panels in Mandeville

More Answers

Do I need a permit to upgrade my electrical panel in Mandeville?

Yes, the City of Mandeville requires an electrical permit, and its application lists service sizes and inspections such as rough-in and final. Work is not to start until the permit is issued, so build the permit time into your project plan.

Do electrical contractors have to register with the City of Mandeville?

Yes, the city says every contractor on an electrical permit must be state licensed and registered with the City of Mandeville. Its electrical permit form also asks for the contractor's state license number. Ask any electrician you hire to confirm both before work on your Mandeville property starts.

What code do Mandeville electrical inspections follow?

The City of Mandeville states that building permits issued on or after February 1, 2018 are inspected to the 2020 National Electric Code. Its Planning and Development page lists the electrical permit form under subcontractor applications. Ask the city about any changes before your project starts, since our licensed electricians work to the code it enforces.

What should I do if an outlet feels warm?

Stop using it, because a warm or discolored outlet cover can signal a loose connection and a fire risk. Turn off that circuit at the breaker panel and have a licensed electrician inspect the outlet before you use it again. Watch for scorch marks or a burning smell too.
