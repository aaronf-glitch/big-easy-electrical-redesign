Which Electrical Problems Should Metairie Owners Have Checked?

Have a licensed electrician check anything that repeats or smells wrong. Flickering lights, breakers that trip again and again, buzzing fixtures and a burning smell near outlets are the problems most worth a call. Each one can point to faulty wiring or an overloaded circuit that should be repaired.

  • Lights that flicker or dim
  • Circuit breakers that trip repeatedly
  • Buzzing or humming from outlets and fixtures
  • A burning smell near outlets
  • GFCI outlets that will not reset
  • Sparking outlets or dead receptacles

About the area

About Metairie

What the community of Metairie is, according to Jefferson Parish's official tourism site.

Lakeshore Community

Metairie is a Jefferson Parish community on Lake Pontchartrain's south shore, set between New Orleans and Kenner. Veterans Memorial Boulevard anchors its commercial life, and Causeway Boulevard serves as the East Bank's business district.

Named for Tenant Farms

The name comes from a French word for a small tenant farm whose rent was paid with a share of the crop. The parish tourism office notes that many early settlers here were sharecroppers.

Fishing Village Roots

Bucktown sits right on the shore of Lake Pontchartrain in Metairie. Formerly a small fishing village, it is now a thriving community with seafood restaurants, markets and a marsh boardwalk.

What is the difference between an electrician and a licensed electrician?

A licensed electrician has completed the training and passed the exams needed to obtain a license. That license shows they can handle electrical installations, repairs and maintenance safely and to code. Our electricians are licensed and insured, and we encourage you to ask any electrician to confirm their license.

Why do all the lights in my house flicker?

When several lights flicker or dim together, the cause is often bigger than a loose bulb. It can point to a loose wire, an overloaded circuit or a panel that is not spreading power evenly. Have a licensed electrician check the wiring and the panel before the problem grows.
