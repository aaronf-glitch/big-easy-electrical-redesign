How we work

What Happens After You Request an Estimate?

We follow up during office hours to arrange a visit at a time that suits you. We assess the site, design the work around your needs and budget, and give you a detailed quote with the cost, timeline and scope before anything starts.

  1. We Follow UpWe contact you during office hours to set up the visit.
  2. Site AssessmentWe visit at a time that suits you and check what your property needs.
  3. Detailed QuoteYou get the cost, timeline and scope of work in writing.
  4. Your CallYou decide whether to go ahead, and we schedule the work around your day.
