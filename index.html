Warning signs

When Should New Orleans Homeowners Call an Electrician?

Call an electrician when you notice warning signs, not after something fails. Fuses that blow often, tripping breakers, flickering lights, a burning smell, hot outlets and buzzing fixtures all point to a problem. A licensed electrician can find the cause and fix it safely.

  • Fuses that blow often, or breakers that keep tripping
  • Lights that flicker or dim
  • A burning smell near outlets, switches or the panel
  • Outlets or switches that feel hot or look discolored
  • Buzzing or crackling sounds from fixtures

How to find a residential electrician?

Start by confirming the electrician is licensed and insured. In New Orleans, electricians must hold a city electrical license and renew it each year. Ask for a site visit and a detailed quote with the cost, timeline and scope before work begins. We offer a free estimate on all services.

Do electricians have to be licensed in Louisiana?

Yes, for many jobs. The Louisiana State Licensing Board for Contractors requires a commercial contractor license for electrical projects over $10,000, including labor and materials. New Orleans also requires every electrician who works in the city to hold a city electrical license and renew it each year.

Do you need a permit for electrical work in New Orleans?

Usually, yes. The City of New Orleans requires an electrical permit to install, repair, replace or alter electrical equipment, except for replacing light bulbs and fuses. Permits must be obtained by licensed electricians, and only a contractor with a city Class A electrical license can obtain one.

Should I upgrade my electrical panel?

An upgrade makes sense when your current panel cannot keep up with what your home or business demands. Breakers that trip often or lights that flicker or dim are common signs. A panel upgrade replaces the old panel with one built for your load, and a licensed electrician should do it.

Can I upgrade my electrical panel myself?

No, a panel upgrade should be done by a licensed electrician for safety and to meet local codes. In New Orleans, the work also needs an electrical permit, and only licensed electricians can obtain one. Our licensed electricians install the new panel to meet local codes.

How long does an electrical installation or repair take?
