Why Install Lightning Protection in New Orleans?

A lightning protection system is designed to protect a building from a direct strike, and especially from the fire a strike can start. It does not stop lightning from striking. Instead, it gives the strike a safe conductive path into the ground, away from the vulnerable parts of the structure.

What Rules Apply to Lightning Protection in New Orleans?

Two sets of rules matter. National safety standards cover how lightning protection systems should be built, and the National Weather Service says a system should follow them. The City of New Orleans also requires an electrical permit to install any electrical apparatus or equipment, except light bulbs and fuses.

The National Weather Service says a complete system needs surge protection devices for incoming power, data and communication lines. Lightning rods alone do not stop surges that enter a building through wires and pipes.

The City of New Orleans states that permits must be obtained by licensed electricians, and it requires electricians working in the city to renew their city license every year. Ask any contractor you hire who will obtain the permit for your system.

Frequently Asked Questions About Lightning Protection in New Orleans

More Answers

Do lightning rods really work?

They protect a building from a direct strike, but they do not prevent lightning. A lightning rod system intercepts a strike and gives the current a conductive path into the ground. That helps protect the structure, especially from a lightning-started fire.

What does a lightning protection system include?

A system includes air terminals, often called lightning rods, plus conductors and grounding rods that carry the charge into the ground. The parts are bonded together to limit sparks and side flashes. A complete system also adds surge protection devices for incoming power, data and communication lines.

Will a lightning rod protect my electronics?

Not by itself, because lightning rods protect the structure while surges can still enter through wires and pipes. The National Weather Service says a complete system needs surge protection for incoming lines, plus protection for vulnerable appliances. Our systems can include surge protectors for that reason.

How does a lightning protection system work?

It gives lightning a path of least resistance. Air terminals catch the strike, conductors carry the current, and grounding rods spread it into the earth. Because the path is planned, the charge is channeled away from the vulnerable parts of the structure instead of through them.

Does a lightning protection system need maintenance?

We maintain lightning protection systems as well as install them, so we can check an existing system or fit a new one. The National Weather Service says the parts should be properly connected, or bonded, to minimize the chances of sparks or side flashes.

Can an electrician install lightning protection?

Ours can: we install and maintain lightning protection systems for homes and businesses across Greater New Orleans. Any system should follow the national safety standards for lightning protection. Our systems can include surge protectors, and our electricians are licensed and insured.
