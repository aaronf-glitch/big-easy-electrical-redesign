Warning signs

What Are the Signs You Need Electrical Repair in New Orleans?

Call for a repair when part of your system acts up more than once. Breakers that trip often, lights that flicker, outlets that stop working, and any sparks or overheating are the common signs. Outlets and switches can also wear out or come loose over time, which creates safety hazards.

  • Circuit breakers that trip often
  • Lights that flicker or dim
  • Outlets that stop working or feel loose
  • Sparks or signs of overheating at outlets or switches
  • A light switch that has stopped working properly
  • A burning smell near outlets, switches or the panel

Local know-how

Do Electrical Repairs in New Orleans Need a Permit?

Most do. The City of New Orleans requires an electrical permit to install, repair, replace or alter any electrical equipment, except replacing light bulbs and fuses. Permits must be obtained by licensed electricians, so repairs to wiring, outlets or breakers call for one.

The city also states that only an electrical contractor with a city Class A electrical license can obtain a permit. It requires electricians who work in the city to hold a city electrical license and renew it every year.

Before you hire anyone for a repair, ask whether they hold a city license and who will obtain the permit.

Is it safe to do electrical repairs yourself?

Usually not. Attempting electrical repairs without proper training and experience can be dangerous and can lead to further damage. In New Orleans, repairing electrical equipment needs a permit, except replacing bulbs and fuses, and only a licensed electrician can obtain one.

When should I call an electrician for a repair?

Call when you see a problem you are not qualified or confident to handle yourself. Frequent breaker trips, flickering lights, outlets that do not work, and any sparks or overheating are clear signals. Bringing in a licensed electrician early can keep a small fault from becoming a bigger problem.

Do electricians repair commercial electrical problems?

Yes. We handle electrical repairs for homes and commercial properties across Greater New Orleans, including faulty wiring and broken outlets, switches and circuit breakers. Can an electrician fix faulty wiring?

Yes. Faulty wiring is one of the core repairs we handle, alongside broken outlets, switches and breakers that keep tripping. Many electrical problems share a root cause, such as an overloaded circuit, aging wiring or a faulty component. We trace that cause first, then quote the repair and complete it to safety codes.
