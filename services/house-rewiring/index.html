Warning signs

What Are the Signs a New Orleans House Needs Rewiring?

The clearest signs come from the system itself. Shocks or tingling at a switch, dimming or flickering lights, tripping breakers and blown fuses all point to wiring trouble. So does a burning or smoky smell, and any of these calls for a professional assessment.

  • Shocks or tingling when you touch an appliance or switch
  • Lights that dim or flicker
  • Breakers that trip often, or fuses that blow
  • A burning or smoky smell
  • Outdated wiring that struggles with modern appliances

Local know-how

Do You Need a Permit for House Rewiring in New Orleans?

Yes. The City of New Orleans requires an electrical permit to install, repair, replace or alter electrical equipment, and rewiring replaces a building's wiring. The city states that permits must be obtained by licensed electricians, and only by a contractor with a city Class A electrical license.

The city also requires every electrician working in New Orleans to hold a city electrical license, renewed each year. Before a rewiring job starts, ask the electrician you hire whether they hold that license and who will obtain the permit.

Frequently Asked Questions About House Rewiring in New Orleans

More Answers

How do I know if my house needs to be rewired?

Watch for repeat electrical problems: flickering lights, breakers that trip often, shocks from switches or a burning smell. Outdated wiring that cannot keep up with modern appliances is another reason. If you notice any of these, have a licensed electrician assess your wiring before the problem grows.

Can I rewire my own house?

Hire a licensed electrician instead. The city requires an electrical permit to replace or alter electrical equipment, and its permit page says permits must be obtained by licensed electricians. Rewiring without the right training also risks shocks and fires, and a licensed electrician keeps the work safe and permitted.

What happens during a house rewiring?

It starts with an assessment of your existing wiring and a plan that sets out the scope and timeline. Then the old wires come out and new ones go in, following safety protocols and local codes. The job ends with testing and inspection to confirm everything works.

What are the benefits of rewiring a house?

Rewiring improves safety, lowers the risk of electrical fires and lets your system handle modern appliances and electronics. It can also improve energy efficiency. An up-to-date system can reassure buyers if you sell, since it meets current safety standards.

Does rewiring include new outlets and switches?

It can. During a rewiring job, we can also upgrade electrical panels, outlets and switches to meet modern standards. We decide what to replace during the assessment and list it in the scope of your quote, so you know what is included.

Do electricians rewire commercial buildings?

Yes, we rewire both residential and commercial buildings in Greater New Orleans. Commercial rewiring follows the same order: an assessment, a plan with scope and timeline, new wiring installed to local codes, then testing and inspection. We schedule the work to cause the least disruption to your business.
