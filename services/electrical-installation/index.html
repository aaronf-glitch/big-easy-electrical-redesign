Local know-how

What Permits Does Electrical Installation in New Orleans Need?

Most installations need an electrical permit from the City of New Orleans. The city requires one to install, repair, replace or alter any electrical apparatus or equipment, except replacing light bulbs and fuses. Permits must be obtained by licensed electricians for work done in the city.

The city also states that a permit can only be obtained by an electrical contractor holding a city Class A electrical license. It requires electricians who work in the city to obtain a city electrical license and renew it every year.

At the state level, the Louisiana State Licensing Board for Contractors issues licenses to electrical contractors. Before any installation starts, ask the contractor you hire who will obtain the permit and which city license they hold.

Frequently Asked Questions About Electrical Installation in New Orleans

More Answers

What does an electrical installation include?

It covers the wiring, fixtures, outlets and switches that carry power through a building, plus lighting systems. For homes, it can also mean new circuit breakers; for businesses, it can include wiring, lighting and panel upgrades. Every building has its own electrical system, so a site visit comes first and the quote reflects what your space needs.

Do I need a permit to install a light fixture in New Orleans?

Yes, in most cases. The city requires an electrical permit to install, repair, replace or alter electrical equipment, except replacing light bulbs and fuses. A licensed electrician must obtain the permit, so hiring one keeps the job within city rules from the start.

Can an electrician install wiring in new construction?

Yes. We handle electrical installation for new construction and renovations in homes, and for offices, retail stores and restaurants. After a site visit and a detailed quote, we schedule the work around your build and install to safety codes, with care and precision.

Can an electrician add more outlets to a room?

Yes. Adding outlets is part of electrical installation, along with fixtures, switches and circuit breakers for homes. The City of New Orleans requires a permit to install electrical equipment, so ask any electrician you hire about the permit. On a site visit, we assess the room, then quote the cost, timeline and scope before any work starts.
