Local know-how

Do You Need a Permit for a Panel Upgrade in New Orleans?

Yes. The City of New Orleans requires an electrical permit to install, repair, replace or alter electrical equipment, and a panel upgrade replaces one. The city states that permits must be obtained by licensed electricians, and only by a contractor holding a city Class A electrical license.

The city sets permit fees by the complexity of the work, and new service connections are one of the factors it lists. Electricians working in the city must also obtain a city electrical license and renew it every year.

A panel upgrade is not a do-it-yourself job, both for safety and to meet local codes. Before work starts, ask the contractor you hire who will obtain the permit.

Frequently Asked Questions About Panel Upgrades in New Orleans

More Answers

How do I know if my electrical panel is outdated?

Flickering lights, breakers that trip often, frequent circuit overloads and a panel that struggles when you add appliances are common signs. Have a licensed electrician assess it, since an outdated panel may not meet current safety standards. It can also pose a fire hazard, so check it before you add more load.

When is an electrical panel upgrade necessary?

An upgrade is usually needed when circuits overload often, when the panel is outdated, or when you plan to add new appliances or rooms. An assessment shows if your current panel can handle the load or needs replacing. We check the panel, talk through what you plan to add, and quote the cost, timeline and scope before anything changes.

Will upgrading my electrical panel increase my home's value?

It can help, but it is not a sure thing. An upgraded panel improves safety, and homebuyers increasingly look for homes with updated electrical systems. The effect on resale value varies, so treat it as a benefit rather than the main reason to upgrade.
