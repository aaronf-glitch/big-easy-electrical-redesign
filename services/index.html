Why Does a City License Matter for Electrical Services in New Orleans?

New Orleans requires electricians who work in the city to hold a city electrical license and renew it every year. Most electrical work also needs a permit, and only licensed electricians can obtain one. That is why the electrician on your job should hold a city license.

What services do electricians provide?

Electricians install, repair and upgrade the wiring, outlets, switches, fixtures and panels that power a building. Our services cover electrical installation, repairs, house rewiring, panel upgrades, troubleshooting and lightning protection systems. We do this work for homes and commercial properties across Greater New Orleans.

What is the difference between electrical repairs and troubleshooting?

Troubleshooting finds the cause of a problem, and a repair fixes it. When a breaker keeps tripping or an outlet stops working, the cause is not always obvious. We trace the fault first, then repair the wiring, outlet, switch or breaker that is causing it.

Do you work on commercial properties in New Orleans?

Yes. We provide electrical services for commercial properties as well as homes, including installation, repairs and maintenance. Wiring, lighting and power distribution all fall within our commercial work. Every commercial job starts with a site assessment and a detailed quote.

Is there a charge for an estimate?
